# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Wednesday, 25 November 2026

A broadsheet newspaper summing up 25 November 2026 in one image: 36 verified
stories across 6 regions, drawn entirely in flat vector with PIL.

- **4 large heroes:** Cartagena supermarket siege, CIA + ODNI cyberattack, Iowa
  lead-azide detonation (9,500 lbs, biggest blast in ~50 years), NATO space defense.
- **8 more heroes:** Russia accuses Ukraine of "terrorism", South Korea minister
  resigns, Australia–Solomon Islands pact, Vaibhav's 204 off 53 balls, Dutch court
  keeps climate targets binding, Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, COP31 ends
  without a deal, Gaza death toll 74,746.
- **Map (bottom left):** every story plotted at its true lon/lat — clear
  continents with coastlines + islands, 36 numbered dots with leader lines where
  they collide.
- **Also on Wednesday:** stories 13–36 in two columns.
- **The Day in Numbers:** 30 hostages, 9,500 lbs, 74,746, 266M, 204 runs, 10 of 12.
- **Date Check:** stories rejected for the wrong date, listed honestly.

`build.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG). `main.py` writes it to
`/output/entry.png`. Every story happened or was first reported on
25 November 2026; research cutoff ~06:15 UTC.
